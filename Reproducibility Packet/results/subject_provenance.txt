A host subject from a laboratory that contributed no donor templates
separates host and donor at the laboratory and institution level as well
as at the animal level. Different institutions necessarily exclude one
shared physical acquisition rig, but these fields do not identify rig
hardware or establish whether the laboratories used the same rig design.
This is still not source independence: both sides remain one dandiset,
one consortium, one acquisition program and one probe type.
