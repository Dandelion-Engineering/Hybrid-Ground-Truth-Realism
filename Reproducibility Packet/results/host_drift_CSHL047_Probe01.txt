  How to read the bound, including where it is not exact:
  - Per bin it is the attainable set, not an approximation of one: a median is
    nondecreasing in every argument, so driving the missing values below every
    observed depth minimises it and above every observed depth maximises it.
  - Above the bin it is an OUTER bound. The same missing values enter a bin
    median and the per-unit centring constant subtracted from it, and interval
    arithmetic ignores that dependence. The error runs one way: too wide,
    never too narrow. This layer can call a candidate unmeasurable that a
    dependence-aware treatment would have called stable; it cannot pass one
    that some completion would have failed.
  - The Q95_null bound is assumption-free. The approved null's permutation is
    drawn from a seed and from the analysed-bin SPIKE count, and a spike whose
    depth is missing still has a good time, so both are fixed before any
    missing value is chosen and the unknown values sit in known positions.
  - The finite-only Q95_null printed above is NOT one of those completions when
    anything is missing: it permutes the observed spikes where every
    completion permutes all of them. It is the number the gate itself reads,
    and it is not claimed to lie inside the bound.
  - An unbounded side is reported as unbounded and makes the candidate
    unmeasurable. No completion places a value at infinity; what an unbounded
    side asserts is that every finite value on it is attainable.

## Boundaries on this measurement

  - This measures drift as visible in IBL's per-spike centre-of-mass depths, not
    physical probe displacement, and it does not separate probe movement from
    tissue movement.
  - The gate holds at the bin grid's 60-second resolution. It neither bounds
    sub-minute motion nor is reliably blind to it; how much of a brief episode
    reaches a bin median depends on the within-bin depth distribution and on
    where the episode falls on the minute grid.
  - The result is conditional on the pre-declared label-blind unit set and on
    movement being expressed in enough of those depth traces for the across-unit
    median to carry it. The per-unit values above do not discharge that
    conditional in either direction.
  - Q95_null is a conservative resolution diagnostic where the additive
    common-movement picture is credible, not a proved bound on the no-drift
    noise floor, and it bounds no systematic bias in the depth estimator.
