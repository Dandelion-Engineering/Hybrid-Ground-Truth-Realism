## Boundaries on these numbers

- The native unit counts are IBL's own Kilosort 2.5 output as published in
  the processed NWB, not this project's sorting. They are the right density
  available first-party density reference for this recording; they are not
  ground truth and do not become a host-admission threshold by themselves.
- The amplitude column is the units table's median spike amplitude in
  microvolts, computed by IBL on their own preprocessed data. Whether its
  convention matches the donor library's amplitude_uv column has not been
  verified, so the comparison against the 50-200 uV rescaling target is a
  flag for that check, not a finding about it.
- Purity is measured over contacts the CCF label map can name. An unmapped
  host label inside a band counts against purity rather than being ignored.
- 'cumulative_drift_um_per_hour' is present in these files and is not used.
  Its values reach millions of micrometres per hour, so whatever it
  accumulates it is not net probe drift, and drift remains an open gate.
