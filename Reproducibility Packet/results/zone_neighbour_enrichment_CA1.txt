Compare the two matchings on the realized count, not on the ratio. Blocking raises
the number of zone partners because it shrinks each candidate set to a neighbourhood
the zone's own members already occupy; part of that rise is what a region-blind draw
under the same blocking would give anyway, which is what the expectation line is for.
The ratio to expectation can fall while the realized contamination rises, and it is
the realized count that dilutes the manipulation.

Read this as a statement about the pull, not about the outcome. A matcher that is
blind to region is not thereby neutral about it: if zone donors sit at the centre of
the covariate neighbourhood the matcher is searching, the control arm inherits them
unless a rule says otherwise. Whether that rule should exclude them, cap them, or
merely report them is a contract question, not a measurement one.
