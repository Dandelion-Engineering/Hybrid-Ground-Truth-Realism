## What the review settled

- Matching is a deterministic global one-to-one assignment with no reuse, self-edge rejection, exact constrained optimality, complete outputs, and loud failure semantics.
- The exposure schedule and placement law are a separate pre-manifest specification. That specification must decide whether placements are derived independently per occurrence or jointly per block and must pin whichever algorithm becomes decisive.
