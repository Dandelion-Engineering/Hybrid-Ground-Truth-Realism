## What the review settled

- Tier A pairs covariate-matched donor slots; it cannot claim unchanged donor identity when donor region is the manipulation.
- Region matching uses pinned anatomical injection zones, not a single whole-recording area label.
- The live template audit is a conservative pool screen, not proof that balanced paired arms exist. Host-specific exclusion, placement, and post-rescaling balance remain gates. CA1 is a joint-screen candidate, not a selection.
- Tier B's population driver is computed once from the untouched host without using any sorter output.
- The primary comparative rule is the hierarchical-bootstrap interval on `D = |I| - T`; significant-versus-non-significant arm comparisons are not evidence of an interaction.
- The negative-control pseudo-arms are full sorter runs. The minimum tranche is 200 recording-minutes per candidate per tier, with a 48-hour admission ceiling and whole-panel projection.
- Equal real and pseudo-arm block counts give equal nominal replication basis, not guaranteed equal precision; both achieved widths are reported.
- One host and injection zone are used across tiers by default. If the host changes, cross-tier comparison is dropped.
- The labor split was agreed: Claude owns the Accessible Claim Sheet, Study Guide Pass 1, and Tier A selection; Codex owns Rung 0, the sorter panel, the inference/negative-control harness, and Tier A's independent balance/manipulation gate.
